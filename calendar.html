Every week

The standing
meetings.

These do not change. The location does — it's announced the day of the meeting by a cabinet member or an adviser, so keep an eye out or just ask.

TUE
11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

The bigger of the two. Setup, catching up, then working on whatever the club is building this semester. If you are coming for the first time, come to a Tuesday.

FRI
11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Looser. More one-on-one help, more experimenting, more of people just playing. Good day to bring a question you've been stuck on all week.

    How a year goes

    The rhythm of
    a semester.

    Fall

    New faces every August. The first month is mostly onboarding — handing out guitars, teaching first chords, finding out what everyone wants to play. By October the club is deep in a set list and playing Halloween events on campus. December closes with a concert.

    Spring

    The Campfire Strum Along takes the club outside to Trinity Plaza — fire pit, PA, lyrics on a screen, anybody who wants the mic gets the mic. Then the semester concert, then officer elections in May, and the whole thing starts over.

    The one to circle

    Campfire Strum Along

    The club's signature night. Outdoors, open to everyone on campus, instruments encouraged but not required. Bring a guitar, bring a friend, or bring nothing and sing. It is the single best introduction to what this club is.

    See you Tuesday.

    Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to bring. Just show up and somebody will hand you a guitar.
