Song Starter

Six questions.
One song idea.

Tell it what you can play and what you want it to sound like. It hands back a progression that real songs are built on, the fingerings for your hands, a strum, and a button that plays it.

Start here

What are you
working with?

Nothing here is graded and nothing is saved. Answer honestly about what your hands can do today — the tool works around it. Every progression below is one that real songs use. None of it is made up on the spot.

1 How many chords do you want?

Two is a real answer. Plenty of great songs never use a third.

2 What can your hands do?

Open chords are the ones with open strings ringing — G, C, D, Em, Am. If barre chords still buzz, say so.

3 Do you have a capo?

This is the big one. A capo lets you play open shapes in keys that would otherwise need barre chords. The club has spares.

4 What should it feel like?

Each of these points at a different harmonic trick, not just major versus minor.

5 Any particular style?

Genre mostly lives in the strum, not the chords. This sets your rhythm and tempo too.

6 What key?

If you are singing it, the key that suits your voice beats the key that suits your hands. That is what the capo is for.

What to do with it

A progression is not a song yet.

Four chords in a loop is a foundation, not a finished thing. What turns it into music is everything the generator can’t decide for you: where you put the emphasis, which chord you hang on an extra bar, what you sing over it, where you stop playing entirely.

The fastest way to use this: draw a progression, play the loop until your hands stop thinking about it, then start changing one thing at a time. Hold the first chord twice as long. Cut the last chord early. Hum something over the top and follow wherever it goes. Nothing here is precious — hit Another one as often as you want.

Bring whatever you end up with to a Tuesday or Friday meeting. Somebody will know what to do with it, and Dr. Héctor A. Torres González can tell you what you accidentally invented and what it is called.
